Conference Booklet

I had the opportunity to make a General Conference booklet. I decided to make a booklet for the talk "The Prodigal & the Road That Leads Home" by Elder Uchtdorf. To start this project, I listened to the talk, and made a mind map. I then sketched out some thumbnails. I made digital sketches and figured out what typeface to use for the project. I made a grid for the booklet to know where to place elements. I then found some colors that went with the book and talk. Finally, I made some mockups of some of the spreads.
